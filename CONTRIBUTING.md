# Contributing

Thanks for helping. This list is only as good as the people who keep it accurate.

## What's welcome

- **New resources** — registries, advocacy groups, patient communities, treatment-access programs.
- **Corrections** — dead links, renamed organizations, outdated guidance.
- **Translations** — this guide is most useful in the languages of the people who need it.
- **Lived-experience notes** — what actually helped, clearly marked as personal experience rather than clinical fact.

## What's not

- **Unproven or promotional treatments.** Anything that reads like an advertisement gets removed.
- **Medical advice.** Keep the tone factual and directory-like.
- **Personal medical details** about anyone other than yourself, and only if you choose to share them.

## How to contribute

1. Open an **issue** describing the change, or
2. Open a **pull request** with the edit.

For a new resource, please include:

- The name of the organization or resource
- A working URL
- One sentence on what it does
- Who it's for (patients, families, clinicians, researchers)

## Accuracy standard

If you are unsure whether something is accurate, say so in the PR. Flagging uncertainty is better than asserting something wrong — especially in a document people may act on.

## Tone

Write for someone who just got the diagnosis and is scared. Plain language, no jargon where a plain word will do, and no false hope.
